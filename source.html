Three Licenses Explained

1. Code Files — MIT License

.html, .css, .js → Freely modify, adapt, rebuild, use commercially. Must include original copyright notice.

2. Documentation & Text Files — CC BY-NC 4.0

README.md, guides, non-display text → Share with credit. No commercial use. No selling or placing behind ads.

3. Page Content — CC BY-NC-ND 4.0

Text visitors read on the website → No rewriting, no adaptation, no commercial use. Must share exactly as-is with credit.
